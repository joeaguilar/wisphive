## Agent-specific learnings
- The blitz self-closed the sprint epic + #399 without running /sprint-review. Correct work, wrong authority — epic close + per-story PO acceptance belong to the review ceremony. Future blitz runs should close stories but leave the sprint-epic close to /sprint-review.
- Stop-and-report is working: the #437 agent hit the wire gap and escalated (did not silently patch the backend out of its owned files). Keep and reinforce.
- Out-of-owned-file additive wiring was handled well: logged as an intervention, diff-reviewed, tagged to the story, and kept — a good template for "make the feature real" edits that cross declared ownership.

## Notes for future /sprint runs
- When a sprint splits a feature into a data-layer story + a UI story, spell out the exact wire/field contract between them in both ACs at plan time (the #434/#437 `tool_input` case).
- For web-view sprints, treat "changes the default view/route" as a signal to run e2e before close.
- The dynamic-smoke-with-CLI-oracle pattern (itr#438: invariants + `wisphive audit` cross-check on non-deterministic live data) worked well and is worth reusing for future runtime-evidence stories.
