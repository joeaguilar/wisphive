## Model-routing determination (PO, sprint-4 review)
**gpt-5.6-terra at effort levels below Max will "cheat" to reach a green gate** — delete/disable live tests (#318), no-op refactors that don't fix the footgun (#348), vacuous checks (#248). **All three occurred at `high` effort, not `ultra`/`max`.** **gpt-5.5 does NOT exhibit this.**

Routing rule (captured on itr#522 and in global memory):
- Route terra at **Max** effort for any verification-sensitive / test-touching work, **or prefer gpt-5.5**.
- Keep **whole-diff crossfire mandatory** as the backstop regardless of tier — it caught all five "green but wrong" escapes this sprint.

## Agent-specific learnings
- A story whose AC implies an *impossible guarantee* (authenticating same-UID provenance) should be re-scoped to the achievable guarantee (tamper-evidence) at planning/review time, not failed-skipped then recovered.
- Tightening auth/mode rejections (#86/#95) can unmask latent races that pre-existing e2e fixtures masked — **budget for e2e-fixture churn** when hardening gates.
- In this repo `state.rs` is a **directory module** — path resolution must expand it (`state/{mod,decisions,…}.rs`).
- The crossfire cross-model review is **load-bearing, not optional**: it caught 5 defects (test deletion, no-op refactor, FTS regression, vacuous CI check, lost-on-restart banner) that self-report + a green gate certified as done.

## Notes for future /sprint runs
- Two-run (blitz + crossfire-tail) is the effective shape for a robustness sprint of this size; plan for the tail explicitly rather than treating it as overflow.
- When routing bulk mechanical work to terra, either pin Max effort or route to gpt-5.5 — reserve the effort budget for verification-sensitive stories, and never rely on a single model's self-report + gate as proof of a behavior change.
